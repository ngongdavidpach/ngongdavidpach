How PropTech works

No badge without boots on the ground

Anyone can post a photo on WhatsApp. Only a real visit earns our shield. Here’s the journey every listing takes.

1

2

The verification standard

What the green shield actually means

  • An agent stood there.

    A named PropTech agent physically visited — not a phone call, not a screenshot from the owner.

  • The map pin is the real place.

    GPS is captured on-site and must land within 50 m of the listed address, or the listing is rejected.

  • Photos are provably fresh.

    Timestamped during the visit — never recycled from last year’s WhatsApp group.

  • Paperwork exists.

    Title deed, allocation letter or chief’s attestation sighted — plus a duplicate-listing scam screen.
