FAQ

Verification questions, answered

What exactly happens during the agent visit?

The agent meets you (or your caretaker) at the property, walks every room or boundary, captures GPS coordinates standing on site, takes a full timestamped photo set, and sights your ownership documents. The visit usually takes 25–40 minutes. Same evening, the verification report is compiled and your badge goes live.

What documents count as proof of ownership?

For formal titles: a registered title deed or lease certificate. For customary/community land (common around Juba): a boma or block chief’s attestation letter, which we photograph and counter-sign with two neighbours where possible. We list chief-attested land clearly labelled as community-title — never dressed up as registered title.

What if my listing fails verification?

You pay nothing. We explain exactly what failed (wrong location pin, missing papers, occupancy dispute…) and you can re-submit for free within 30 days once it’s fixed. Sellers who repeatedly submit fake listings are permanently barred and flagged to the community watchlist.

How do I pay the $25 badge fee?

Only after a successful visit — never before, never online. Pay the agent directly by mGurush, Zain Cash, MTN Mobile Money, or cash with a printed receipt. Any “PropTech agent” asking for money before a visit is a fraudster: report them on +211 915 234 800.

How long does the badge last?

90 days. After that, listings become stale (tenancy changes, water situation changes…). Re-verification costs $15 and takes one short visit. If the property is rented or sold in the meantime, the listing is simply retired.

Does PropTech guarantee the tenant or the property?

We guarantee the verification, not the people: that the property exists, matches the photos, sits at the shown location, and that the person listing it had credible authority at the visit date. We’re present to mediate if a verified listing misleads you — report it and we re-inspect within 72 hours. Contracts and rent stay between the two parties.
