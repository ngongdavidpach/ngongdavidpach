The four checks behind every badge

  • Physical site visit

    An agent — salaried, never paid per approval — walks the property with the owner or caretaker.

  • GPS truth test

    The location captured on the agent’s phone must land within 50 m of the location the owner claimed. Bigger gaps are auto-flagged for a phone call, and approving a 200 m+ gap is blocked outright.

  • Fresh photo evidence

    Photos are shot on the agent’s device during the visit. Recycled marketing shots don’t make it into a verification report.

  • Paper trail

    Title deed, allocation letter or chief’s attestation sighted, plus a duplicate-listing and fraud-watchlist screen before anything goes live.
