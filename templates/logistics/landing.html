How it works

1

Drop a pin

Customer opens the app and taps the map for pickup and drop-off. No street names needed.

2

Instant fare

Distance-based pricing in SSP, shown before you confirm. Pay cash or m-Gurush on delivery.

3

Nearest rider

Job is dispatched to the closest verified boda rider in real time. Driver app shows Google Maps navigation.

4

Track & confirm

WhatsApp alerts at every step. Recipient shows a 6-digit handover PIN and the parcel is delivered — proof photo included.
